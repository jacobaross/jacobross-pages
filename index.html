I grew up in Vermont, and now live in New York City with my wife and our two kids.

I spent the last seven years at PebblePost, first as President, then as CEO. PebblePost is a commerce media platform that uses a massive household data graph to reach consumers through direct mail and connected TV and drives incremental revenue. It's a unique business. I stepped down earlier this year to take a kind of sabbatical: recharge, reconnect and write the next chapter.

Before that, I worked in SaaS and marketing technology for over a decade, including as GM and Chief Product Officer at MediaMath and General Manager of North America at Criteo.

I'm currently advising companies and leaders, building multi-agent systems for myself and others with AI, and exploring what's next.

I'm interested in technology, data, markets, and understanding what truly moves the needle. I consider myself a growth operator at heart, and enjoy building things fast enough to find out if they're right, doing it with passionate people and making an impact on the world.
